The first step taken when starting a project. Planning is how you figure out what you need to accomplish - generally just identifying end goal and the steps needed to get to there. 
To start planning, you need to find your [[Mission Statement]] and [[Vision Statement]].
[[P.O.L.C]] the framework.

## Here are types of plans:
**Strategic**: Long term, around 5-7 years.
**Tactical/Operating**: short term, 1 year
**Single-use**: this plan is only used once
**Scenario**: plans that can have multiple different endings
**Contingency**: Plan B, if unexpected happens

## Planning Tools:
1. [[Strategic Management]]: look in the future, develop strategic plan
2. [[Project Management]]: specific, focused on achieving a specific goal
