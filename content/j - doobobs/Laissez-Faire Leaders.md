Subset of [[Leading]], one of the [[Leadership Styles]] in its own special way. in the [[Blake and Moulton - Management Grid]]
Laissez-Faire Leaders let the workers direct themselves. They are the high-level organizers, and expect lower levels to get the job done and orient themselves. Sometimes called "weak leaders" since all of their power is pushed on to subordinates. very laidback and disassociated.
