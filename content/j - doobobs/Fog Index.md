A tool to measure the number of years in schooling required to understand / read a passage. 
The Formula is:
([Average Words Per Sentence] + [% of long words (3+ syllables)]) x 0.4
That gives you the number of schooling required to understand something.

This is not foolproof, but it's a pretty handy tool for tailoring your writing to specific groups.
For example, educated professionals will be okay with passages that score higher on the Fog Index, while high school students might need a relatively lower Fog Index score to understand.
