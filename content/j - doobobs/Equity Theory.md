Equity theory believes that people don't judge rewards absolutely, but rather in relation to what other people receive. In this, there is a concept called the 'referent'. A referent is someone who is similar to you (same job, same age, same qualities, etc). When one receives a reward (bonus, praise), they often look to the referent to see if what they got was 'fair'. 
Example: Someone gets a $2K bonus and is happy.  They go talk to their coworkers to share the good news, only to find out that everyone else got a $10K bonus. Despite the money amount not changing, the employee now feels that their reward was 'insufficient', because it wasn't fair compared to his referent group (the other employees). 
Equity Theory also believes that people value fairness over anything, to the point where they will also feel angered/unwell when THEY are the ones who get the unfair prize.

People don't know everything about their referent, so they often incorporate bias into their reasoning.

To remove conflict in the workforce, discourage sharing of salaries and other outputs.
