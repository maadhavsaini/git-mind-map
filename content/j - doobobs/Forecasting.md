### Plan A: Regression Model
> when there is past history to look at

look at the trendline of the past to see the future. 

### Plan B: Quantifiable Simulation
> when there isn't any past history

Use an algo to determine course of action, usually compared to test results of a decision before action is taken. Can be very complex and expensive.

### Plan C: Guess!
> Sometimes in the business world you will have to guess...use the [[Delphi Method]] when guessing

remember: ALL FORECASTS ARE STILL GUESSES 😥
