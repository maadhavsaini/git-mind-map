So you know [[Goods]] right? now imagine goods required in order to produce those goods. They are physical assets that a business uses to produce goods or services for others. These are used to make the things people buy!!!
Characteristics of Capital Goods:
1. Durability: they are long-term assets that last for years
2. Man-Made: created by humans, not natural resources
3. Productive Value: sole purpose is to increase production or provide a service
Examples include: Machinery, factories, delivery trucks, computers, etc etc
they are also very high value and require significant Capital Expenditure (CapEx) to purchase

When a capital good gets in bad shape and could break, you must work on [[Maintaining the Capital Good]].

When you can't get the capital good you need, you might need to consider finding alternatives.
