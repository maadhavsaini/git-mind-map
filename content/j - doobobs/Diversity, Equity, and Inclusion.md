Works to create a fairer, more representative and welcoming environment for all employees. Primarily an HR function.
**Diversity**: should be different types of people within a given setting, like race, gender, age, religion, and even ways of thinking
**Equity**: ensures that everyone gets what they need to succeed
>[!Question] Why isn't it 'equality'?
>Equality is giving everyone the same thing, Equity is giving everyone what they *need* to succeed

**Inclusion**: ensuring people feel a sense of belonging and support from the organization.
