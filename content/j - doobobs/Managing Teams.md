> [!warning] RULES (by Sun Tzu)
> 1. Never form a new team where an old team can be used
> 2. Make teams as small as possible
> 3. Provide support to teams as a manager

Now, when teams are too cohesive, you gotta break it up to prevent [[Groupthink]]. That's when people value other's feelings over the right thing to do. So, play devil's advocate. challenge the team's assumptions, question it. Note too that groupthink can happen with long-standing teams, so periodically check up on folks. For [[Social Loafing]], create individual performance metrics and regularly check up on individuals. This gets everyone on the same page - that you matter 🤗. 
Crossfunctional teams can solve big, massive problems, but it will take a long time to form storm and norm due to their diverse backgrounds and little similarity. Only add as much functional diversity as needed to reduce impact of similarity.
