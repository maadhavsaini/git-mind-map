A document that provides a detailed description of specific work to be done under a contract. explains how the work is done. Leaves no ambiguity.
'Acceptance Criteria' are the specific requirements/conditions/standards a deliverable must meet before the client accepts and pays for it. 
basically acceptance criteria is conditions for project approval.
For a criterion to be effective, it must be:
1. Measurable - specific quantifiable measurements
2. Testable - way to prove the criteria is met
3. Binary - yes or no (criteria met or not met)

Example of something cool to say: "We need to refer back to the **Statement of Work** to see if the specific **Deliverables** and **Service Standards** were met. If they weren't, we have the grounds to withhold the final **Milestone Payment**."
