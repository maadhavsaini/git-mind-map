## Verbal Behaviours - When Speaking to Another
- **Tone of voice** — Professional, confident, and appropriately enthusiastic. Avoid sounding monotone or too casual.
- **Volume** — Loud enough to be heard clearly, but not overpowering.
- **Pace/Rate of speech** — Not too fast (nervous) or too slow (uncertain). Steady and controlled.
- **Clarity & Enunciation** — Pronouncing words clearly; avoiding mumbling.
- **Vocabulary** — Using business/industry-appropriate language. Avoid filler words like _"um," "like," "uh."_
- **Active listening responses** — Verbal acknowledgments like _"That's a great point,"_ or _"I understand your concern"_ show engagement.
- **Persuasive language** — Using confident, assertive statements rather than weak hedging like _"I think maybe..."_

## Nonverbal Behaviour (this was all made by Claude btw)
- **Eye contact** — One of the most important factors. Maintain consistent eye contact with the judge; it signals confidence and trustworthiness.
- **Posture** — Stand or sit up straight. Slouching signals disinterest or lack of confidence.
- **Facial expressions** — Smile naturally; express enthusiasm and engagement. Match your expression to the context.
- **Gestures** — Use purposeful hand gestures to emphasize points. Avoid fidgeting, crossing arms, or touching your face.
- **Proximity/Space** — Maintain professional distance from the judge (not too close, not backing away).
- **Appearance** — DECA requires **business professional attire**. Your presentation starts before you speak.
- **Movement** — Controlled, deliberate movement. Pacing nervously or staying completely rigid are both negatives.
