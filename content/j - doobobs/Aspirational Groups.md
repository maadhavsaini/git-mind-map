One of the [[Social Groups]].
An aspirational group is a group that a person wants to join but currently isn't in. Similar to [[Reference (Referent) Groups]], except that the person actively wants to belong to that group instead of just following what they say and do.
Examples include an amateur athlete following the regime and diet of Olympic athletes so that he can become an Olympian.
