One of the [[Economic Systems]].
A blend of [[Market Economy]] principles, like private ownership, competition, and profit motive, with government intervention in certain services and areas. Virtually every modern country uses this. Its just too tuff ✌️
Governments step into:
1. Regulations - minimum wage, food safety laws, environmental laws, etc etc
2. Public services - schools, hospitals, roads, military
3. Taxes - to fund social programs, redistribute wealth
4. Subsidies - support sustainable industries, farmers, energy corps, domestic companies
5. Antitrust laws - breaking up monopolies to protect competition and encourage growth
They do it because pure markets can lead to monopolies, inequalities in wealth, public goods being neglected, and other external forces.

Singapore is more market-oriented, the US and Canada are in the middle, while Germany and Sweden have more government-involvement.
