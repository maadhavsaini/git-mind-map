This is an example of [[Risk Management]], where an individual or business pays a **PREMIUM** for protection against specific financial losses or risks. Specifically, this is [Risk Transference], as you are transferring the financial obligation to a 3rd party.
> Paying a little to avoid losing a lot

## Key Terms
**Premium**: regular payment made at specific intervals
**Deductible**: what you pay yourself before insurance covers other stuff
**Claim**: formal request to get the insurer to pay for a covered loss
**Policy**: the contract outlining coverage terms
**Liability**: legal responsibility to others for damages
