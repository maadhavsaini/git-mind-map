## Types of Needs
### Hygiene Factors:
> The bare minimum. Can't motivate, but will demotivate if not provided. Once you reach the baseline, additional increases. extrinsic.
> Examples: company policies, supervision, working conditions, salary, security
### Motivators:
> unlimited ability to motivate people. extrinsic.
> Examples: recognition, achievement, increased responsibility, growth and advancement

Making luxury bathrooms does not increase motivation.
