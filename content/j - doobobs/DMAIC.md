Framework often used by [[Six Sigma]] to analyze existing processes and improve upon them. 
DMAIC stands for:

**Define** - identify problem, goals, customer requirements
**Measure** - collect data on current process
**Analyze** - use data, find cause of defects/problems
**Improve** - develop, test, implement solutions to eliminate cause of problems
**Control** - monitor new process to ensure that the improvements sustain over time
