> [!abstract] Definition: Motivation is the intuition of achieving a goal, leading to goal-directed behavior.

## Theories of Motivation
These seek to explain why people are motivated.

| Needs-Based                                               | Process-Based                                                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Individuals want what satisfies their own needs. Selfish. | Individuals rationally choose the best choice for them, based on the situation |

## Types of Motivation
| **Intrinsic Motivation**                                                                                                                                                                                   | **Extrinsic Motivation**                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Individuals do something to find an inner feeling of accomplishment. An example would be volunteers, as they are motivated knowing that they did something important. Personal satisfaction is found here. | To earn a reward, a bonus, a certificate, praise, recognition, to be popular. External rewards motivate the person to adopt goal-directed behaviour. |
