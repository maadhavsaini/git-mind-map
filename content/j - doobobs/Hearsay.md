An out-of-court statement used to prove truth. You'd think it's not allowed, since it was said outside, but NO. There are MANY exception, so just note this:
Courts care about reliability. Hearsay could be allowed in a business law context if it can be proven that it was said, it is relevant, and many other factors that could possibly have this be relevant.
