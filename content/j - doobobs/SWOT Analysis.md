Way to understand a business's position in the world and their market. Draw a square split into 4 squares: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.

| *Internal*:    | Strengths         | Weaknesses  |
| -------------- | ----------------- | ----------- |
| ***External*** | **Opportunities** | **Threats** |
Write your company's (or personal) strengths (what you do good), weaknesses (what you lack in), opportunities (what you could expand in), and threats (what could undermine you).
