One of the [[Social Groups]].
A dissociative group is a group that a person does not want to be affiliated with. They will actively disengage from participating with this group, and change their behaviors and habits so that they are not identified as belonging to this group.
Examples: an employee not hanging out with his coworkers since they have a reputation of being lazy, a consumer not buying a specific brand due to them being associated with something the consumer doesn't like.
