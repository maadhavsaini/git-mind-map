A tool that allows you to identify the discrepancies between your project results and its original plan. 
## Three Core Pillars:
1. Current States - realistic assessment of where you at rn
2. Future State - where you wanted to be
3. The Gap - the area for improvement

You have to identify target (future state), analyze current situations, identify the gap, and develop a plan.
Uses cases include [[Market Gap]], [[Skills Gap]], [[Profit Gap]]
