Laws are ruled established by a governing authority to regulate behavior.
Laws are:
- enforceable
- applies to everyone
- maintains order
- protects rights
- resolves disputes

## Types of Law
1 -  Criminal Law
- deals with crimes against society
- ex: theft, fraud
- government will prosecute, jail time
2 - Civil Law
- disputes between individuals / businesses
- TORTS!!! ex: contracts, negligence
- usually about money & damages

## Sources of Law
Federal - the US Supreme Court
State - state courts
Local - municipal governance.
