Keeping a business's productive assets in good condition so that they can continue to work. Not letting important and productive resources wear out. 
[[Capital Goods]] need to last a long time, thus maintaining its lifespan is necessary. 
Maintaining could mean:
- Cleaning the good
- Being very careful when using it
- Repairing it regularly
- Replacing it before it becomes unusable
