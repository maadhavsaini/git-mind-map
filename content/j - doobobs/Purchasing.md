how you make sure you get the supplies needed to continue operating and functioning. Purchasing have some steps:
1. Identifying Needs - what do you need to run the business?
2. Selecting Suppliers - choose vendors that see contracts as a way to develop and grow together. Analyze, do market research to find the best ones suited to your needs
3. Placing Orders - use [[Purchase Orders]] and all of its subsets to get the stuff you need
4. Evaluating Supplier Performance - make sure they are upholding their part of the contract, doing what you need them to do
