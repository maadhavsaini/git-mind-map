Project management tool - divides complex projects into smaller, manageable components. Takes 100% of the total scope and splits into, focusing on deliverables.

Goal is to define all deliverables, clarify scope, and facilitate planning, organizing resources, and controlling. 

WBS Dictionary provides details like responsibilities, risks, and milestones for each element.

WBS has many levels, most projects use 3-5. Typical levels here:
0. Final Deliverable
   This is the top of the hierarchy and represents the final, complete deliverable. This is the goal. Everything below this level must add up to 100% of the [[ Project Scope]].
1. Major Deliverables or Phases
   The project is split into smaller phases of completion, like design, development, testing, deployment, etc. 
2. Sub-deliverables or Key Tasks
   Each Level 1 deliverable is broken down into sub-deliverables/key tasks. These are output-oriented, help assign responsibility to teams (each team does one sub-deliverable or something)
3. Work Packages
   The smallest units of scope that can be assigned to a person or team. Each work package has clear deliverables, timelines, owners, and required resources so that it can be tracked.
4. Activities (optional)
   The exact steps a person would perform, often used in detailed scheduling tools like [[Gantt Charts]] or [[Project Software]].

The higher levels are strategic and show what will be done, lower are practical and show how it is done.
