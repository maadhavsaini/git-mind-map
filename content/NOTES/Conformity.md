Conformity is the tendency to behave in the same way everyone else does instead of thinking and speaking up for yourself. Very common in  [[Groupthink]], where people choose not to speak up because they are shy, unwilling to create conflict, or don't think they know enough to speak. This means that different opinions, which are necessary to create a good final decision, are not voiced and thus lead to a bad decision.
