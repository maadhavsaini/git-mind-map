Net Promoter Score (NPS) is a widely used customer loyalty metric that measures how likely customers are to recommend your product/service/company to another. It's simply: "On a scale of 0 to 10, how likely are you to recommend [company/product] to a friend or colleague?"


| Category    | Rating | Behavior                                                                    |
| ----------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Promoters   | 9 - 10 | Loyal enthusiasts who keep buying and actively refer others, driving growth |
| Passives    | 7 - 8  | Satisfied but unenthusiastic, vulnerable to competitive offers              |
| Distractors | 0 - 6  | Unhappy customers who can damage your brand through negative word of mouth  |

Overall NPS = % of promoters - % of distractors. Scores range from -100 (every respondent is a detractor) to +100 (every respondent is a promoter)

Overall measure customer loyalty and identify issues. are used with customer touchpoints.
