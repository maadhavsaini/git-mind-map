**Telling**: you care more about getting the job done than employees. You tell them what to do and make them do it
**Participating**: you care a lot about people and want them to be involved, but more carefree on tasks
**Delegating**: you give away responsibility to others to do - weak leader - give tasks and expect them to do it
**Selling**: 'sell' the task to people and care about them - get the task done and care about the employee

As you can see, the leadership studies linear progress plot onto the Matrix depending on which areas they are high in vs low in.
