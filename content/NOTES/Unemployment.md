## Types of Unemployment
1. Structural - long-term problem, your skills no longer match available jobs. technology could automate jobs, industry declines, etc. Requires retraining and education
2. Technological - 
3. Frictional - temporary, occurs when people are between jobs. this isn't a problem, it always occurs
4. Cyclical - unemployment caused by economic recessions (2008 financial crisis led to low demand, so businesses cut millions of jobs). goes up in recessions, down in booms
5. Seasonal Unemployment - jobs only exist a certain time of the year (lifeguards in summer, ski instructors in winter)
6. Disguised Unemployment - people work but aren't productive/needed (5 people working on a farm where only 3 are needed). - common in developing countries, leads to low productivity
7. Underemployment - person working below their skill level due to a lack of full-time or skilled jobs (uni grad student working at McDonald's)
8. Long-term Unemployment - unemployed for 6+ months , leads to a decline in skills, makes it harder to re-enter workforce
