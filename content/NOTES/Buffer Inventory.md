Buffer inventory is extra inventory kept on hand in case of an emergency regarding supply chains, demand, etc. It's a safeguard to prevent any supply issues, ensure there is enough inventory to meet demand and fulfill orders on time.

Buffer inventory takes up time and oftentimes capital as well. Businesses need to plan how much buffer inventory to keep on hand to minimize costs while still preventing shortages. Strategies like [[Just-in-Time]] (JIT) reduce buffer inventory to minimal/zero, focusing more on receiving goods when ordered.

Buffer inventory is how businesses play it safe and work around [[Risk|risk]].
