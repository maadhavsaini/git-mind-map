a business has a responsibility to society beyond just making profit. must act ethical, and contribute positively to its community.

## FOUR MAIN AREAS OF CSR
**Economic Responsibility**
- you still need to be profitable
- stay sustainable as a business, so you last long

**Legal Responsibility**
- follow laws ([[General Data Protection Regulation]], labor laws, data laws, etc)

**Ethical Responsibility**
- do whats RIGHT, even if not required by [[Law]] (follow the letter and spirit of the law)
- ex: fair wages, honest marketing

**Philanthropic Responsibility**
- give back to the people(donations, volunteering, community programs)

–
CSR is everything.
