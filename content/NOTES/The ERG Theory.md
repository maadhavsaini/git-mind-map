Made by [[Clayton Alderfer]], the ERG system seeks to improve upon what [[Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs]] lacks - complexity. As mentioned in that note, the Hierarchy is too simple and is not that realistic. ts under [[Organizing]]

The ERG Theory takes Maslow's Needs and groups them into three categories:
1. **Existence**
   In Maslow's: Physiological & Security
   aka Deficiency Needs
2. **Relatedness**
   In Maslow's: Social
3. **Growth**
   In Maslow's: Esteem & S.A. (👀)

> [!note] ERG says that needs are NOT hierarchical, and that one can be [[Motivation|motivated]] by different needs at the same time. 
> Sometimes in the real world, a LOWER need is sacrificed since there's another, more important one (subjectively). Example: student skips lunch (physiological need) because they have to prepare for a presentation (esteem, since they want to be the best presentation).

Basically, although Maslow's Hierarchy is simple, famous, and easy to understand, ERG is better at explaining the motivations of real people.
