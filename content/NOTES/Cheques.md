# Writing a Cheque

> [!NOTE] When writing a check...
> Use black or blue ink on a cheque from your bank-issued chequebook
1. Date it current or post-date (future date instead of today, banks won't process cheque until that date arrives or after – good for rent, bills, loans)
2. Write payee name (person receiving money) starting left on "Pay to the Order of" line
3. Write number amount in the box
4. Write amount in words (spell out value – "one hundred dollars and 50/100) %%50/100 means 50¢, add cents as fraction%%
5. OPTIONAL: Memo line (short explanation – "Rent - April '26)
6. Signature

# Cashing / Depositing a Cheque
Endorse the back with your signature, then deposit.
