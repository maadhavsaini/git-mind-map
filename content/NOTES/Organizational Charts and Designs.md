subset of [[Organizing]]. Org Charts show communication between roles and [[Authority]]. However, org charts don't tell you what the role's responsibilities are, the quality of [[Managers]], etc etc.

## Classic Line and Staff Organization
![[Pasted image 20260414095240.png]]


note how HR reports directly to the CEO. This shows departments that can bypass the hierarchy and work directly with higher management when needed. Also, a 'dotted line relationship' is when you bring in someone from another team to work with you on a project. They report to you but aren't permanently under you.
