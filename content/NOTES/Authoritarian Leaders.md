Tags: #roles #concept 

Subset of [[Leading]], one of the [[Leadership Styles]].
These leaders take minimal to zero input from followers, and use their own intuition, logic, and knowledge to get the job done OR plan out what others do. They are the ones who focus more on the TASK rathar than the person.
Most (in)famous Authoritarian: Joseph Stalin.
- He worked to be the sole decision maker on all major activities of the USSR
- His word became law, and he suppressed anyone who digressed from his opinion
- His iron fist ended up strangling him; his doctors were too scared to help him since they thought he'd accuse them of trying to kill him (not relevant, just a fun fact)
