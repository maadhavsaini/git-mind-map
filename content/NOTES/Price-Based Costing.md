Strategy where a company sets a target cost based on what the market is willing to pay.
1. determine how much customers will pay for a certain topic
2. determine profit margin and deduct that from market price to get the allowed cost of production
3. engineer product, source material, and optimize operations so that the costs (fixed and variable) of making the product stay below the allowable cost
