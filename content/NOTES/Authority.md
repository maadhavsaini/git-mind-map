All organizations have formal and informal authorities. When groups are made between employees, they supervise and group themselves.
## Some Related Topics in Authority
[[Chain of Command]] (aka Line of Authority)
[[Unity of Command]]

## Formal Authority
Authority given by the [[Organizational Charts and Designs|org chart]], org structure, and company policy. Clearly developed by the business, you will find this on an org chart.

## Informal Authority
Authority given by people to people based on seniority, reputation, personal influence, and personal ability. You will NOT find this on an org chart. Not clearly developed by the org, this happens naturally (see first paragraph).
