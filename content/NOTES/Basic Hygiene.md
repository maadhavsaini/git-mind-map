Basic Hygiene is one of the most important qualities to maintain as a professional, as it directly impacts your overall personal appearance. Through maintaining your appearance, you represent the company better and show that you are a responsible person who can take care of themselves - no one wants a disheveled, unkempt person to be a brand ambassador.
Helps with maintaning your health and physical well-being.
