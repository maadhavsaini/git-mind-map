The last thing to do when conducting a [[Project]]. Controlling is how you ensure that you and your company is going in the correct direction. To check your current movement, there are many different types of control.
Major ones are:
1. Negative Feedback: when you get negative feedback, you are doing the wrong thing and need to change. Examples are: sales drop after a controversial price change, it takes you longer to finish your homework when you have the TV on
2. Positive Feedback: you are doing the right thing, continue on this path. Examples are: more people talk to you when you shower, you get higher grades when you study for 30m a day
   this could amplify what you are doing btw
3. Feedforward Control: proactive way to avoid problems instead of reacting to them
4. Feedback Control: you do something and get the results - reactive
5. Concurrent Control: real-time guidance / corrections while the thing is going on

See [[Closeness of Coupling]] for more stuff


A control system needs a Goal and Feedback loop.

> [!tip] you need both positive and negative feedback in order to actually do the right thing
