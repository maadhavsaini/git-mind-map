## How to meet each in the workplace:
**Physiological** - provide a safe, comfortable work area. Provide food, drink, and make it security
**Security & Safety** - provide a salary, health insurance, retirement pension, and give them trust that you will pay them
**Social** - provide team-building activities, office parties, social gatherings
**Esteem** - provide titles, recognition, parking spot, distinguishing perks
**Self Actualization** - (can be pretty hard) provide growth and experience opportunities to 'level up'

> [!warning] however, there's a problem with Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs
> **IT'S TOO SIMPLE** 
> Maslow's Theory assumes that
> 1. Individuals are forced to satisfy their lowest desirable
> 2. Individuals can only be at one level at a time
> This is not realistic. Although Maslow's Hierarchy provides a great foundation of the types of needs that motivate an individual, there's another concept that is able to make it more applicable in the real world: [[The ERG Theory]]
