These folks:
- have a vision
- are confident in themselves
- can explain and articulate
- have a Magnetic Personality
	- powerful self-confidence
	- persuade others to follow them

> example of transformational leader: Steven Jobs[^1], and Mahatma Gandhi

## 4 Major Tools
*Inspirational Motivation*
- has vision of future & the guts to make it happen
*Intellectual Stimulation*
- thinking past the norms and considerations
*Individualist Consideration*
- care about the people they lead
*Charisma*
- inspire confidence & love from followers

They can be [[Democratic Leaders]] or [[Authoritarian Leaders]], not laissez-faire. why? because when leading change, YOU push forwards and lead others, you don't let the confused lead themselves.
