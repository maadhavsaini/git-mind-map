Made by [[Michael Porter]], this is also known as "Porter's Diamond".
![[Screenshot 2026-04-12 at 3.05.04 PM.png]]
These 5 forces show the way external changes can affect your business and shape your [[Strategy]]. 

Way to understand is not just to look at individual comp, but the WHOLE industry of companies selling products. the way they change is a key tool for defining [[Competitive Advantage]].

> [!warning] KNOW THIS
> If Seller power increase, they can charge higher prices. If buyer power increases, they can demand lower prices
> New competitors makes business management harder
> Development of substitutes challenges your products
> Intensifying rivalry is when demand goes down, markets mature, businesses have to fight harder and harder for smaller and smaller returns. This shapes yo competitive advantage.
