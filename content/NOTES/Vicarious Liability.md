This is a legal doctrine holding that an employer is legally responsibly for the actions of an employee, as long as the actions were done within the scope of employement (wrongful actions like negligence were done in a workplace setting, regarding workplace duties, etc). Now under this, there are distinctions.
1. Detour
   A *minor* deviation was done by the employee, but it's still technically under the scope of employment (delivery driver took the wrong turn and spent 2 minutes going back on route, hit a car doing so)
2. Frolic
   A *major* deviation was done by the employee, to the point where it's out of the scope of employment (delivery driver went to his friends house in company vehicle to hang out, hit a car along the way)
