Concept made by [[Henri Fayol]], states that each employee should only have one person that they report to. Why? It removes confusion for the employee, as they now have to wonder who is their boss and who's work to prioritize. No need for that. By having only one superior, it ensures that tasks from the boss are given straightforward and clearly.
If you see [[Organizational Charts and Designs]], notice that Matrix Orgs have poor Unity of Command. This is because employees are part of a role group and a task group, and thus have to report to both and fulfill the requirements of both. This isn't really ideal. Simply put, aim to only have one direct superior to each employee. Each superior can have multiple people below them, however ([[Span of Control]]). That's fine.
