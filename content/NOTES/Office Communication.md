Goes under [[Organizing]], as well as [[Authority]] subset.
Communication at the office happens in many forms between roles of the office.

**Lateral Communication**: comms between people on the same level of the org hierarchy
**Vertical Communication**: comms from executives down through to the lower levels, or employees up to managers. Follows the [[Chain of Command]].
**Grapevine Communication**: informal gossip, rumours, secrets between employees.

Formal AND Informal comms AND authority are very important in an organization.
