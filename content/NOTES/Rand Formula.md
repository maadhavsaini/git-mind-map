Canadian labour [[Law]] principle - requires union dues to be paid by all employees covered by a collective agreement, even if they are not union members. If they get the benefits, they pay for it. Introduced by Justice Ivan Rand in 1946 after the Ford strike in Windsor, ON.
AKA automatic / compulsory check-off. Purpose is, as mentioned before, to ensure that those who benefit from union contracts pay their dues.
The employer deducts union dues from employee's pay automatically.
