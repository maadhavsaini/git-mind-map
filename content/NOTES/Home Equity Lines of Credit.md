You put your home as collateral to get [[Revolving Credit]]. you can borrow, repay, borrow again up to a set limit. 

**Pros**: very flexible, low interest (since your house is on the line), only pay interest on what you withdraw
**Cons**: your house is on the line. variable rates, so monthly payments can increase as interest does too.
