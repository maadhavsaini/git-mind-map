COBRA
___
Instructional Area: [[Business Law]], [[Human Resources Management]]
Tags: #concept 

This is a U.S. federal law, allows employees to temporarily keep their health insurance coverage after they leave a job.
This is a benefit program.
Basically: it is the exact same insurance plan the employee had while working...however...
Under COBRA, individual must pay the full premium themselves, instead of the company paying for part of the insurance. This makes it more expensive, but allows the employee to continue the plan after they leave the company.
Companies with 20 or more employees are legally required to offer COBRA to employees. this has to do with [[Severance Packages]].
