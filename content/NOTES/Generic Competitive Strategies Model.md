this diagram is a way for businesses to analyze which [[Strategy]] they must adopt.
**Differentiation**: High-cost but unique. Your product is NOT the cheapest, but it has specific features that make it "better" than others. Examples: Ferrari, Apple, Orange Theory
**Cost Leadership**: Low-cost, commodity. Your product is the CHEAPEST. Examples: Walmart, Dollarama, Spirit Airlines.

In ANY market, there are many differentiated competitors yet only one or two cost leaders. This is because only the cheapest businesses can boast about their low prices, not the 3rd or 10th cheapest. Thus, other competitors are forced to utilize other factors apart from price to make their product desirable. That makes you DIFFERENTIATED.
