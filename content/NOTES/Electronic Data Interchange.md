Instructional Area: [[Operations]]
Tags: #concept 

The sending of business documents (invoices, purchase orders) electronically, in a standardized format.
Sender translates docs into an EDI format, like [[X12]] or [[EDIFACT]]. They transmit securely via [[VAN]]/[[AS2]], and receiver auto-processes document into their system.
Related to [[Purchase Orders]], especially [[Blanket Purchase Orders]], since those documents are often sent via EDI.
