Your personal appearance is the way you look and present yourself to others. Maintaining a professional personal appearance is very important, especially as an employee, because you are essentially a representative of your company. See [[Vicarious Liability]]. 

So as an employee, your appearance reflects the image of the company. If you look and act professional, customers and vendors are likely to think higher of your business. On the contrary, if you are disheveled and unkempt, customers and vendors will think badly of your company, as they associate you with the business. It is a matter of correlation.
