A [[Situational Leaders|Situational Leadership]] strategy. This asserts that:
- your leadership style is FIXED!! CANNOT CHANGE!!! 
- you are naturally positioned towards ONE style: either [[#Task-Oriented]] or [[#Relationship-Oriented]].
- you can identify your style with the following activity (Least Preferred Co-Worker (LPC)). Take your least favored colleague, and then rate them. If your rating of them is pretty high despite them being someone who you don't like, you are a [[#Relationship-Oriented]] individual. If you rate them pretty low, you are a [[#Task-Oriented]] individual.
- looks at "situational favourableness": how easy or hard it is for a leader to influence their team. yadda yadda blah blah something about Leader-Member relations, Task Structure and Position Power yadda yadda high or low for each yadda yadda
