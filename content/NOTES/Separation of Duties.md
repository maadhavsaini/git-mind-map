The idea that no single person should have the power to complete a business process from start to finish. Prevents error and fraud. 
For any major financial or operational process, these functions should be handled by different people:
1. Authorization: The person who approves the transaction
2. Custody: The person who has physical access to assets
3. Recording: The person who logs the transaction in accounts

Without SoD, it's possible for fraud like [[Disbursement Fraud]] to happen within a company, conducted by employees.
