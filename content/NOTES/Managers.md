Managers are integral to a business. They are very important to a business's functions. But managers don't just sit alone and plan - the majority of their duty & time is spent in conversation with others. 
Niche stats: 
- avg manager spends 75% of their time with others (subordinates, bosses, others)
- most of their work is done through verbal communication, formal & informal

## Roles of Managers
1. **Interpersonal Roles**
   Managers interact with many people. Through their work with others, they fulfill the role of a leader with subordinates. 
   
   > [!example] For example, Lee Iacocca turned Chrysler in the 1980s from a bankrupt, near-extinct corporation back to the great auto manufacturer they once were. Another example is the legendary [[Henri Fayol]], who took over a failing steel company and turned it back to profitability (using the P.O.L.C framework)
   
1. **Informational Roles**
   Managers need to take in and give out many kinds of information. They become information centres. Businesses often install information/[[Knowledge Management]] systems to manage company info, but a manager's head is the fastest and most reliable.
   As a gatherer, managers look to gather information from others through various channels. this includes all types of [[Office Communication]].
   As a disseminator, managers have to pass on information to subordinates. In doing so, they have to decide what to pass on, how much to let known, and how to tell their subordinates.
   As a spokesperson, managers sent information to people outside of the org (executive making a public speech, supervisor talking to a potential supplier). This also includes the media, as they often ask managers to give both factual and opinions to be sent out to the world. If done effectively, they can gain brand recognition.
2. **Decisional Roles**
   Managers need to make many decisions on a day to day basis, as well as major ones that could set the course of the business's future. There are many ways one would make a decision ([[Delphi Method]], [[Forecasting]], etc)
