## Types of Teams
1. Cross-Functional - team members from throughout the company work together to solve a problem. Low similarity. use when problem is too big to be addressed by one function only.
2. Functional - team members from the same background (all accounting, IT) work together to solve a problem. High similarity and stability
3. Self-Managed: teams elect a leader to manage the work process from within. can take a long time to set up and get a problem, but they have high cohesion
