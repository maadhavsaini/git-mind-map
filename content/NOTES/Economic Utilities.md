An economic utility is the value a consumer gets from a product. There are five types of economic utilities:

1. **Form Utility**
   turning raw materials into a useful good.
2. **Time Utility**
   represents the value of getting something *when* customer needs it (24-hour pharmacies, seasonal items, etc)
3. **Place Utility**
   represents the value of getting something *where* customer needs it (local convenience store, nearby grocery store)
4. **Possession Utility**
   represents the ease of getting a product to actually own and use. This can be done through flexible payment methods and quick shipping (prime same-day delivery)
5. **Information Utility**
   aka Knowledge Utility, this relates to giving customer necessary detail and information on using a product and why they should buy it
