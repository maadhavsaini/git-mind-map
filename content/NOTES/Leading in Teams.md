## Stages of Group Work
==this isn't linear, it goes back and forth often== - known as Punctured Equilibrium
![[Pasted image 20260421155042.png]]

Groups are very cohesive when they pertain towards:
1. Similar - people of similar background
2. Stability - no underlying circumstances (TMUN Germany 👀)
3. Size - not good when too big, depends when too small
4. Support - how they can support each others
5. Satisfaction - if there're happy within the group

## Problems of Working in Teams
1. Groupthink
   individual ideas aren't said / recognized in order to keep everyone happy
2. Social Loafing
   people don't put in effort since they think they won't be recognized individually whether they put in the work or not
3. Conformity
   no new ideas, everyone kinda just goes with the flow
