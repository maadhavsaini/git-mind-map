Before you start, know what a [[Referent]] is.
This is one of the [[Social Groups]].
A reference group is a group that a person looks up to and uses to evaluate themselves. The person doesn't have to be a part of the group, but the person looks to the reference group to know how to act, look or think.
Example: A medical student copying the dress and speak of surgeons, since they want ot be a surgeon when they grow up.
