Instructional Area: [[Strategic Management]]
Tags: #concept 

Subset of [[Organizing]]
Here, key decisions are made at the top (lower workers don't make important changes).
[[Middle Managers]] don't have high authority & need to get upper management to make decisions. Not flexible, but cheap. This is why [[Cost Leadership]]-oriented businesses always prefer centralized organizations.
Popular when you have a few product lines. Not flexible, may not work in unstable markets.
[[Managers]] are expected to be motivators and followers.
