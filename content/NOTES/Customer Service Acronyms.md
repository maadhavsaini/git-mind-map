# H.E.A.T
###### used widely for customer de-escalation

1. **H**ear them out - let the customer vent, dont interrupt
2. **E**mpathize - acknowledge their frustration, validate their anger
3. **A**pologize - offer a sincere apology for their poor experience
4. **T**ake action - outline next steps and work to resolve the issue. even make a timeline

# L.A.S.T
###### direct, highly efficient framework to resolve mistakes

1. **L**isten - Pay full attention to the issue
2. **A**pologize - accept responsibility on behalf of your org
3. **S**olve - fix the issue promptly and accurately
4. **T**hank - thank customer for bringing up the issue
