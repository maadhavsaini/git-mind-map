Subset of [[Credit]].
You receive a service upfront (immediately) and agree to pay the full balance at the end of a billing cycle (usually monthly). You pay in **full** every month, you cannot roll over a balance (not similar to [[Revolving Credit]]). 
AKA Service Credit (since you receive a service, then pay for it after)
Examples: utility bills (electricity, water), mobile phone plans
