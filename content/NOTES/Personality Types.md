In order to properly discern the information provided in this module, it is of the utmost importance for the reader to familiarize themselves with that which is known as "Trait Theory". 

## Trait Theory
asserts that our personalities determine our [[Leading]] style.

## The Big 5 - OCEAN
ideally you want high in everything except for Neuroticism
**O**penness - thrive in flexibility, intellectual
**C**onscientiousness - high attention to detail, this predicts a person's success in mgmt
**E**xtraversion - good in speaking
**A**greeableness - forgiving, fair, nice with others
**N**euroticism - trouble forming relationships, not a trusted person, paranoia

other personality types that are to a lesser extent important:
self-monitoring - regulate themselves in social groups, identify strengths and weaknesses
proactive personality - works to fix problems before they start
self-esteem - are confident in themselves
self-efficacy - believe that they are successful in a task, improve performance

anyone who has strong leadership skills can become a Trait Leader - someone who can command people with intrinsic emotional appeal. Personality Traits and behavior make up a successful leader
