Retirement account where amount of money contributed is specified (defined 👀) , but eventual benefit you receive at retirement is not guaranteed. Final amount for requirements depends on how much you put in, and sometimes employers also match contributions. You choose where that money is invested. Also the money grows tax-deferred, and taxes are only paid when you withdraw money in requirements.
What gemini says on how DC works:

- **Contributions:** You decide to put a percentage of your paycheck (e.g., 5%) into the account before taxes are taken out.
    
- **Employer Match:** Many companies will "match" your contribution up to a certain limit (e.g., they put in $1 for every $1 you put in, up to 3% of your salary). This is essentially "free money."
    
- **Investment:** You choose how to invest that money (stocks, bonds, or mutual funds).
    
- **Growth:** The money grows tax-deferred, meaning you don't pay taxes on the gains until you withdraw it in retirement.
