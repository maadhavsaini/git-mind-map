A goal is a objective/milestone that you want to achieve. Goals are made by many, but oftentimes people don't achieve them. To create a goal that will actually stick, follow the SMART Goal format:
**S**pecific - be clear, answer what you want to accomplish
**M**easurable - make criteria for measuring progress
**A**chievable - be realistic, and possible to complete
**R**elevant - ensure the goal actually matters to you
**T**ime-bound - set a deadline to finish by

Other ways of managing goals include writing them out, as it helps you to clarify them in your own mind and provides something you can look back to to keep yourself on target.
