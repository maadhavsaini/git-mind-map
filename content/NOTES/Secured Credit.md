Subset of [[Credit]].
This is more about *how* you get the credit. Opposite is [[Unsecured Credit]].

In this, you put up an item of value (aka collateral) to get the loan. if you do not pay, the bank takes the item (see [[Home Equity Lines of Credit]], mortgages, auto loans).
