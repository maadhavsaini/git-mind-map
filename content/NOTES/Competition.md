The rivalry between companies to win over customers by offering the best combination of price, quality and service. The engine of a free-market economy, since it forces businesses to innovate and keep prices fair.
## Types of Competition
1. Perfect Competition
   Many businesses selling identical products, no single business controls the price. Leads to a need for innovation
2. Monopolistic Competition
   Many businesses selling similar but differentiated products
3. Oligopoly
   A few large companies dominate the market (Bell vs Rogers, Coke vs Pepsi)
4. Monopoly
   One single business controls the entire market with no substitutes (local utility company)

Competition is good because it fosters innovation between companies.
