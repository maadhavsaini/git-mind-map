A brand promise is what a business "promises" to do when interacting with their consumers. Brand promises are kept all the time, to show the business's fairness and equity. 

## Example of Brand Promises:
- **Walmart**: Save money. Live better
- **Nike**: To bring inspiration and innovation to every athlete in the world
- **Disney**: Creating happiness through magical experiences

Brand promises must convey a compelling benefit (why consumers should stick with you), be authentic and credible, and be kept every time.
