Subset of [[Credit]].
This is more about *how* you get the credit. Opposite is [[Secured Credit]].
In this, the loan you get is based purely on your promise to pay, no collateral attached. The bank won't take anything if you fail to pay. Credit score and history will still be impacted, + possibility of debt, but no tangible items are being taken.
