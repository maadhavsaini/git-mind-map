Subset of [[Organizing]], but it's also related to [[Controlling]].
SoC is how many people report to a manager / how many people are directly under a manager.
Increase SoC when employees have similar jobs, it's easy to monitor them all, they are experienced and well trained, already in teams, etc etc.
For employees that you can't physically see (online, other location), use [[Information Technology|IT]] tools! 
A consistent SoC means that everyone has no more than *x* subordinates. Changing the SoC will almost certainly rework the entire organizational hierarchy ([[Organizational Charts and Designs]] reference).

When SoC is HIGH: there aren't that many [[Managers]], organizational chart looks FLAT, managers are very busy (lots of people under them).
![[Pasted image 20260414112752.png]]FLAT ORG up here - only 3 layers in total

When SoC is low, there's more hierachy, and the organization is TALL. Managers aren't very busy, high cost.
![[Pasted image 20260414113221.png]]

> [!tip] ALWAYS HAVE YOUR ORG AS FLAT AS POSSIBLE WITHOUT LOSING CONTROL
> that means the highest SoC that works well with your org
