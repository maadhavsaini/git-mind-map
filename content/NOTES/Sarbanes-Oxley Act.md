SOX
___
Instructional Area(s): [[Business Law]], [[Financial Analysis]]
Tags: #concept 

US federal law, made in 2002. Protects investors from corporate accounting fraud – made in response to scandals like Enron and WorldCom. Mandates strict financial reporting standards, internal controls for public companies. Financial statements must be certified by CEOs and CFOs personally. The [[Public Company Accounting Oversight Board]] oversees auditors and bans non-audit services.
