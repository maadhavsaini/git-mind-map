> [!abstract] Definition: Incentive is something that incites a determinate/action in another

Under [[Organizing]], also good for HR, and has emotional intelligence stuff in it. very multifaceted.
## Types of Incentives
1. Positive Incentive - carrot - it signals that actions taken are correct. reward
2. Negative Incentive - stick - it signals that actions taken are incorrect. punishment

## Reinforcement Theory

| Positive Reinforcement       | Negative Reinforcement    |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Positive Action = Reward     | Positive Action = nothing |
| **Punishment**               | **Extinction**            |
| Negative Action = Punishment | Negative Action = nothing |
NOTE: Positive & Negative Action is what the employee/subordinate/idk does, = *x* is what the manager/you do/does.

NOTE: Positive Reinforcement is much better to do, as it's more effective in the long term. Gets people to do positive actions and not negative.

Closely connected to [[Motivation]].
