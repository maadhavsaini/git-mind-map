Subset of [[Credit]].
With this, you get a set maximum limit of how much you can spend. You can borrow, pay it back, and borrow again up to that limit as long as the account stays open. How much you spend will reset after a period of time, usually every month. You must pay the outstanding credit by a certain point. With this, you could either pay the full balance every month *or* pay a minimum amount and carry interest on the rest (not the best). 
Examples are credit cards, [[Home Equity Lines of Credit]]
