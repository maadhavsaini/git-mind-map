Instructional Area: [[Emotional Intelligence]]
Tags: #concept

Created by Daniel Goleman, Emotional Intelligence explains how people understand and manage the emotions of themselves and others. The four domains are:
1. Self-awareness
   Recognizing your own emotions, strengths and weaknesses, and confidence
2. Self-management
   Controlling impulses, staying motivated under pressure, and remaining adaptable
3. Social awareness
   Understanding the emotions of others and group dynamics
4. Relationship management
   Handling interpersonal communication, teamwork, conflict, leadership
Emotional Intelligence says that these traits can be learned, much unlike IQ. People call Emotional Intelligence EQ, but Goleman prefers EI since it disassociates the concept from the static and immutable IQ.
