By [[Henri Fayol]]. The Chain of Command (aka Line of Authority) details the path from the lowest rank to the highest rank in the business. Instructions passed down from management and critical situations escalated by employees all pass through the Chain of Command. This is also a [[Professional Development]]-related concept, as a hard-working employee could use the CoC to visualize their progression through the company, and see the roles that lie ahead of them.
