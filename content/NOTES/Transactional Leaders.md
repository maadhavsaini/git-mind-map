Transactional leaders focus on rewards and consequences. This is what most people think [[Management]] is.
These guys know the rules and follow them. Hold everyone to the same standard as themselves, and use rewardws and coercive power to [[Controlling|control]].

An Example of A Transactional Leader: **Parents!**
Good parents: 
- set expected standards
- deliver rewards and punishments when needed

Transactional Leaders can be [[Democratic Leaders]], [[Authoritarian Leaders]] or [[Laissez-Faire Leaders]] - but they have to be aligned to rules and ALSO follow them!

**[[Personality Types]] are not important*** - as long as you have integrity and follow rules, you good. whats important is what you do.

> [!warning] RULES
> no hypocrisy
> follow rules
> track performance
> set clear expectations
