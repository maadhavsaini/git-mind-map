> [!example] Examples of Cost Leaders
> Southwest Airlines, Microsoft, Walmart

Cost Leaders seek efficiency and give up flexibility.
The main thing they say is: The products are all super similar, so why not buy the cheapest?
Features of a Cost Leader:
- [[Centralization]]
- They use [[Centralized Organizations]] as their framework for hierarchy.
- Little delegation of [[Authority]], everything decided by higher management
- [[Operations]] and [[Financial Analysis]] are the most important to a Cost Leader, lots of senior [[Managers|managers]] are from these two areas
- Need to hire followers, people who can motivate employees, and managers
- Everyone in the org is focused on cost-cutting, and cost is most closely controlled
- Budgets and spending reports are the most important control tools
- Any capital investments are made to reduce costs
- Cost Leaders utilize [[Economics of Scale]] to their advantage, and thus always strive to grow - they are never big enough
SUMMARY: Cost Leaders focus on reducing costs throughout the board[^1] through standardization, economics of scale, and [[lean]] processes.![[Screenshot 2026-04-13 at 3.07.13 PM.png|322]]By investing in new technologies, businesses move the 'sweet spot' downwards and to the right. This means production volume increases while product unit cost decreases.

Mantra: REDUCE COSTS

[^1]: meaning: reduces costs across all areas of the business
