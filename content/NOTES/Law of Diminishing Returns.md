The law of diminishing returns is a fundamental principle that says as one input increases while others stay the same (ex. workers increase, space and materials stay the same), the output that you get from adding each new input slowly decreases, until it can even get worse.

## Stages of this law
1. Not enough 
   If you add inputs now, you will see huge output increases since you haven't hit the potential yet. At this stage, keep adding inputs.
2. Just right
   At this point, the amount of output generated per unit has started to slow down. It's not in the negatives, but it's making less output then previous inputs. At this stage, stop adding inputs - you've hit the Point of Maximum Yield
3. Too many 
   Here, you have too many inputs and are likely seeing negative output (when you had less inputs, you were making more then now). At this stage, remove inputs until you've hit the Maximum Yield.
