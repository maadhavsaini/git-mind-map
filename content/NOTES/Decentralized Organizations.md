Subset of [[Organizing]]
In decentralized, key decisions are made as low as possible in the hierarchy. Here, [[Middle Managers]] can make decisions without informing the CEO and corporate. This is very flexible, but expensive, which is why [[Differentiation]]-oriented companies use this.
Also good for companies with lots of product types, and those that serve lots of consumer groups. These adapt well to changing markets. See [[Organizational Charts and Designs#Product Organization]] for an example of hierarchy in a decen/ org/. 
[[Managers]] are expected to be problem-solvers.
