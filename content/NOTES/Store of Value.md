Instructional Area: [[Economics]]
Tags: #concept

Any asset, currency or commodity that can be saved today and still hold its purchasing power / lose very little power when you use it later. Goal is to protect against inflation or decay.

EXAMPLES: 
- Gold & Precious metals
- Real estate, stocks, cryptocurrencies
- Money and currency (inflation can erode its real value over time)

KEY QUALITIES OF A GOOD STORE OF VALUE:
- Durability – shouldn't break down or spoil easily
- Scarcity – limited supply keeps value from collapsing
- Acceptability and liquidity – people should recognize its value, should be easy to trade or convert

TL;DR: store of value describes anything you can hold over time to preserve wealth.
