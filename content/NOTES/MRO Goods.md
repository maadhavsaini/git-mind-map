Maintenance, Repair, and Operating Goods
___
Instructional Area: [[Operations]]
Tags: #concept

Supplies, equipment, materials needed for a business to operate, but these aren't sold / part of the final product. to be consumed by the organization.
**Maintenance**: items used to keep the facility in good shape (paint, cleaning supplies)
**Repair**: to fix machinery & infrastructure (valves, screwdrivers)
**Operating**: general supplies needed for daily work (paper, pens, gloves)

Orgs use [[Blanket Purchase Orders]] to get MRO goods. Why? Because you can get it regularly without having to file a PO every single time.
