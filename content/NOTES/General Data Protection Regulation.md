GDPR
___
Instructional Area(s): [[Business Law]]
Tags: #concept

European Union law made in 2018 to protect personal data and privacy of EU residents. Applies to any organization that handles the data of EU citizens. 
"Data processing must follow seven core principles: law body textfulness, fairness, transparency; purpose limitation; data minimization; accuracy; storage limitation; integrity/confidentiality; and accountability."
Personal data includes names, emails, IP addresses, biometrics.
