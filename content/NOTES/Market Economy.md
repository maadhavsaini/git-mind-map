Instructional Area: [[Economics]]
Tags: #concept

One of the [[Economic Systems]].
The decisions on what to produce, how to produce it, and who it is produced for ( [[Three Fundamental Economic Questions]] 👀) 
are made by individuals and businesses, not by the government. The market controls.
Core Idea:[[ Supply and Demand]]. Prices rise and fall based on how much is available (supply) and how much people want it (demand).

Key Features of a market economy:
- private ownership - individuals and companies own property and businesses, not government
- freedom of choice - consumers choose what to buy, businesses what to sell, workers where to work
- competition - multiple businesses compete for the same target market, in theory improves quality and keeps prices fair
- profit motive - the main reason businesses operate is to continue earning
- prices as signals - rising or falling prices signal if suppliers make more or less of a product

[[Adam Smith]]'s invisible hand is a great example. he says that when everyone acts in their own self-interest, markets tend to allocate resources efficiently without anyone coordinating it

Examples of Market Economies in real life:
- United States (kind of mixed, since government regulates and provides some services. however, the private enterprises dominate)
- Canada (similar to the US, with stronger public health systems)
- United Kingdom (market driven with public health system)
- Singapore (free market, low taxes, very business friendly)
- Hong Kong (historically one of the freest economies in the world)
