Customer Information is just information that you get about your customers. This is usually done through questionnaires, surveys, purchase history and more. When it comes to customer information, businesses need to be **very** careful with how they manage it. 

# Types of Data Collected
- personal preferences and communication notes
- general information (name, contact, etc)
- private information (credit card #, address, etc)

# Dangers of Collecting Data
Cambridge Analytica was a consulting firm that harvested the personal data of millions of Facebook users to influence and spread political beliefs. They were directly involved in major political campaigns like Brexit and the 2016 US Elections. This is obviously not good.
