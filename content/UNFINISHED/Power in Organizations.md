All leaders and [[Managers]] exercise power, there are many different styles of power

**Coercive Power** - [[Transformational Leaders]] - power to punish
**Reward Power** - [[Transactional Leaders]] - power to reward
**Referent Power** - [[Personality Types|agreeableness]] - power of being liked, esteemed. ppl want to be your friend 
**Expert Power** - intelligence - power of knowing more about a certain topic
**Information Power** - situational - power of knowing a secret that nobody else knows
**Connection Power** - very political - power of knowing someone with authority

Powerful people have many different types of power, but everyone has some degree of power.
