Hello DECA folk. This is my personal mind map; I've used this starting Grade 9, and this was a huge help in my ICDC preparation for Principles of Business Management. I'm going to continue using this throughout this year as well. I want this to be a reference to you, as my goal is for you to create your own mind map just like this.

When you get a question wrong on Answerwrite, Clustermaxx, decademy, etc, just search the topic and write a quick summary in a new note. If you start early, you can have a map with hundreds of topics to review at any time.

Some suggestions for your Obsidian Vault
1. If you don't know what an Obsidian Vault is, or even what Obsidian is, go ahead and read through the Get Started pages here: https://obsidian.md/help/. 
2. DON'T OVERCOMPLICATE! There's really no need for a perfect setup and tons of extensions, I only use one - Excalidraw. Other than that, nothing. If you overcomplicate things, you're taking time away from studying for competitions.
3. Follow a course. To create this vault, I followed the Modernstates course on Principles of Management (2025 Edition), find the course here: modernstates.org. I made a new note for each topic in the course, and wrote whatever the instructor talked about + additional info on the web. Follow the provided course or any other one, and you'll soon have a huge vault with tons of information. 
4. Don't copy off of me. I want you to use this as a reference, maybe even a study tool if you like it. But when you make your own, don't copy whatever I wrote. Follow your own course and populate it with topics regarding your DECA event.

If you need any more help with Obsidian, you can ask me anytime.
